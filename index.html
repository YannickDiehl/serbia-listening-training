Waitlist-controlled field experiment for social workers

Listening Skills Training in Serbia

• 20 Serbian municipalities (10 experimental, 10 control)
• Four measurement occasions (T0–T3, January–December)
• 451 social workers
• 102 items & 23 preregistered scales
• Three hypothesis blocks (H1 Listening & Togetherness → H2 Cognitive Complexity → H3 Well-being)
