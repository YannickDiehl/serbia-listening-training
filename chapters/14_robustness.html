14. Robustness across sample sets

NoteAbout this chapter

Repeats the training effects on the sample sets S0 to S9: the repeated-measures Δd of Chapter 9 and the preregistered multilevel models of Chapter 10, once for each way of choosing persons and questionnaires, and the Δd also with the standard deviation of all questionnaires before the window. An effect that holds across the sets does not depend on who answered when or on careless answering; the latent effects across the same sets follow in Chapter 11.9. Feeds the robustness table of the supplement.

14.1 The sample sets

14.2 Δd of the composites

Does the descriptive effect hold in every set? The Δd of the three hypothesis composites (Chapter 9), in the four windows:

composites <- c("H1_ListeningTogetherness", "H2_CognitiveComplexity",
                "H3_Wellbeing")
windows <- tribble(
  ~pre, ~post,
  "T0", "T1",
  "T0", "T2",
  "T0", "T3",
  "T2", "T3"
)

# the Δd of `outcomes` in every set and window (sd_pre as in delta_d())
set_effects <- \(windows, outcomes, sd_pre = "pairs") {
  sample_sets |>
    select(set, data) |>
    cross_join(windows) |>
    mutate(table = pmap(list(data, pre, post), \(d, a, b)
      delta_d(d, a, b, outcomes, sd_pre))) |>
    select(set, pre, post, table) |>
    unnest(table) |>
    mutate(window = str_c(pre, " to ", post))
}

14.3 Multilevel models of the composites

Does the preregistered test hold in every set? The interaction terms of the preregistered models (Chapter 10), per set:

interaction_terms <- \(data, outcome) {
  lmer(as.formula(str_c(outcome,
                        " ~ Occasion * condition + (1 | Municipality/id)")),
       data = data) |>
    tidy(effects = "fixed") |>
    filter(str_detect(term, ":")) |>
    transmute(wave = str_extract(term, "T[1-3]"), b = estimate,
              se = std.error, p = p.value)
}

robust_mlm <- sample_sets |>

  cross_join(tibble(outcome = composites)) |>
  mutate(terms = map2(data, outcome, interaction_terms)) |>

  unnest(terms)
