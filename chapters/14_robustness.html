14. Robustness across sample sets

NoteAbout this chapter

Repeats the training effects on the sample sets S0 to S9, effect by effect: the repeated-measures Δd of Chapter 9 for each of the 23 scales and the three hypothesis composites, once for each way of choosing persons and questionnaires, also with the standard deviation of all questionnaires before the window, and for the composites the preregistered multilevel models of Chapter 10. Every outcome goes through the same five steps: Δd in every set, another standard deviation, the multilevel model in every set, the verdict, and what follows. An effect that holds across the sets does not depend on who answered when or on careless answering; the latent effects on the same sets are Step 7 of each construct in Chapter 11. A table at the end puts all effects side by side. Feeds the robustness table of the supplement.

14.1 How each effect is examined

What happens to each effect? The outcomes are the 23 scale scores and the three hypothesis composites (Chapter 2). Each goes through the same five steps, in the same order and in three parts: the effect in every set, the multilevel model, and the verdict. The diagram shows the path; the list below describes each step.

The five steps in detail:

The effect in every set:

  1. Δd in every set. The Δd of Chapter 9 (Step 2): the change of the experimental group minus the change of the control group, each in standard deviations before the window, with its z-test; significant at p < .05. It is computed in each of the nine sample sets (14.2): for the scales in the two training windows, T0 to T1, while the experimental group trains (expected positive), and T2 to T3, while the control group trains (expected negative, as the freshly trained control group gains more); for the composites also from T0 to T2 and from T0 to T3, as in Chapter 9. Δd rests on the persons who answered at both waves of a window, so a set changes it only where it leaves out some of these pairs. A figure shows Δd with its interval in every set, the decision of Step 4 at a glance.
  2. Another standard deviation. The same Δd with the standard deviation of all questionnaires of the group at the first wave of the window (Chapter 9, Step 3). This standard deviation changes with every set that leaves out questionnaires of that wave, even where the pairs stay the same; in S1, where every person answered at every wave, the two nearly coincide. The question is whether a verdict (p < .05) changes.

The multilevel model:

  1. The multilevel model in every set. For the three composites, the preregistered outcomes: the model of Chapter 10, outcome ~ Occasion * condition + (1 | Municipality/id), refitted in each set, with its three interaction terms (the change from T0 to T1, T2 and T3, experimental minus control; expected positive at T1 and T2, and near zero at T3, after both groups have trained). The single scales are fitted once, on all questionnaires, in Chapter 10.

The verdict:

  1. The verdict. In how many of the nine sets the effect is significantly positive and in how many significantly negative, in each window and under both standard deviations (for the composites also in the multilevel model). An effect counts as robust when it holds in the expected direction in all nine sets, under both standard deviations.
  2. What follows. Where the result goes next, and what it amounts to.

The Δd of all outcomes in all sets is computed once, in 14.3; the scales follow by hypothesis block (14.4 to 14.6), then the composites (14.7), and 14.8 sets all effects side by side.

# Δd with its 95 % interval in each set, one panel per window; filled
# points are significant (p < .05), open points are not
plot_sets <- \(effects) {
  effects |>
    mutate(set     = factor(set, levels = rev(sample_sets$set)),
           ci_low  = delta_d - qnorm(.975) * se,
           ci_high = delta_d + qnorm(.975) * se,
           test    = if_else(p < .05, "p < .05", "p ≥ .05")) |>
    ggplot(aes(x = delta_d, y = set, xmin = ci_low, xmax = ci_high,
               colour = test, fill = test)) +
    geom_vline(xintercept = 0, colour = "grey60", linewidth = .3) +
    geom_linerange(linewidth = .5) +
    geom_point(shape = 21, size = 2.2, stroke = .6) +
    facet_wrap(~ window, nrow = 1) +

    scale_fill_manual(values = c("p < .05" = "grey15",
                                 "p ≥ .05" = "white")) +
    labs(x = "Δd, experimental minus control, with 95 % interval",
         y = NULL, colour = NULL, fill = NULL) +

# for a composite, the model of Chapter 10 fitted once more in every set
effect_path <- \(name, next_text) {
  composite <- name %in% composites
  pairs  <- (if (composite) robust_delta_d else robust_scales) |>
    filter(outcome == name)
  all_sd <- (if (composite) robust_delta_d_all else robust_scales_all) |>
    filter(outcome == name)
  # the sets with a significant Δd in the expected direction
  holds <- \(effects, w, sign) {
    sum(effects$window == w & effects$p < .05 & sign * effects$delta_d > 0)
  }
  first  <- c(holds(pairs, "T0 to T1", 1), holds(all_sd, "T0 to T1", 1))
  second <- c(holds(pairs, "T2 to T3", -1), holds(all_sd, "T2 to T3", -1))
  changed <- sum((pairs$p < .05) != (all_sd$p < .05))
  multilevel <- if (composite) {
    t1 <- sample_sets$data |>
      map(\(d) lmer(as.formula(str_c(name, " ~ Occasion * condition + ",
                                      "(1 | Municipality/id)")),
                    data = d) |>
            tidy(effects = "fixed") |>
            filter(term == "OccasionT1:conditionExperimental")) |>
      list_rbind()
    str_c("T1: ", sum(t1$p.value < .05 & t1$estimate > 0),
          " of 9 sets significantly positive")
  } else "fitted once, on all questionnaires (Chapter 10)"
  tribble(
    ~Step, ~Question, ~Result, ~Next,
    "1", "In how many of the nine sets does Δd hold?",
    str_c("T0 to T1: ", first[1], " of 9 positive; T2 to T3: ", second[1],
          " of 9 negative"), "another SD",
    "2", "Same verdicts with the other standard deviation?",
    if (changed == 0) "yes, in every set and window" else {
      str_c("no: ", changed, " verdicts change")
    }, "multilevel model",
    "3", "The multilevel model in every set?", multilevel, "verdict",
    "4", "Robust: all nine sets, both standard deviations?",
    str_c("T0 to T1: ", if (all(first == 9)) "yes" else "no",
          "; T2 to T3: ", if (all(second == 9)) "yes" else "no"),
    "what follows",
    "5", "Where next?", next_text, "")
}

robust_delta_d <- sample_sets |>
  select(set, data) |>
