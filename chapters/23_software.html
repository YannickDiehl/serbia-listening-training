Part A — Data

From Questionnaires to the Analysis File 79 Excel files, code corrections, double entries, and the case log. Scales and Analysis Datasets Reverse coding, scale composites, long and wide data. Sample Participants, waves, municipalities, and sample sets. Data Quality Careless responding, two flags, and the search for double entries.

Part B — Measurement

Reliability and Baseline Correlations Reliability per wave and correlations at baseline. Measurement Models per Wave Confirmatory factor models at each wave (T0–T3). Measurement Invariance over Time Configural, metric, and scalar invariance across the waves. Simultaneous Block Models Block CFAs: discriminant validity and block-level invariance.

Part C — Training Effects (Preregistered)

Means and Δd Means by condition and wave, and standardized change. Multilevel Models Treatment effects, replication, and sensitivity. Latent Difference-in-Differences Latent DiD in structured means models. Serial Mediation Serial mediation chain: H1 → H2 → H3. Movers Classification of individual change. Robustness across Sample Sets The training effects on the sample sets S0 to S9.

Part D — Post Hoc

Item Pruning Reduction and cleanup of the item pool. Exploratory Graph Analysis Dimensional structure via network psychometrics. Redundancy & IRT Redundancy analysis (UVA) and graded response models. Final Structure The final item pool, its communities, factors and composites. Training Effects on the New Structure Δd, multilevel models and mediation on the two factors. Synthesis Integration of all findings.

Part E — Appendix

Item-Level Δd The training effects item by item, ranked. Software Versions R, Quarto and every package with its version. Requests and Their Status What the team asked for, in plain words, and where it stands.
